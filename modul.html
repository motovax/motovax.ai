Pilih modul Motovax untuk
operasional dealer mobil Anda.

Motovax menghubungkan stok unit, inbox, CRM, konten, finance dealer, dan laporan. Agen AI dapat memakai fungsi pada modul aktif untuk membantu sales, call center, marketing, serta management dari data dealer yang sama.

M1

Inventory Management System

Stok multi-cabang real-time: unit, foto, import Excel, status ready/booked/sold.

  • Daftar & detail unit, filter cabang
  • Upload / import Excel + validasi
  • Foto unit & worker perawatan media
  • Query & laporan stok lewat AI WhatsApp
Demo: Sistem Manajemen Inventaris
M2

Agentic AI (WhatsApp)

Agen Falcon / Jasmine dengan tool native untuk melayani pelanggan dan operasional internal.

  • Bukan keyword bot — LLM + tool schema
  • Simulasi kredit, TNS/GP, trade-in
  • Handoff ke admin, call center, MR
  • Sales lead, registrasi user & login dashboard
  • Caption, posting promo & stock alert
Settings: Integrasi WhatsApp · Meta · Instagram
M3

AI Omnichannel & Call Center

Inbox WA + Messenger + Instagram: AI 24/7, takeover manusia, handoff MR.

  • Faneling AI / agent / MR + tag Cold–Hot–Booking
  • Realtime SSE (tanpa polling)
  • Aksi cepat: inventori & simulasi
  • Auto-routing, pin, bulk close & morning briefing
Demo: Call Center
M4

Autopilot CRM

Pipeline, customer, MR, follow-up otomatis, guideline, dan agent workspace dalam satu alur.

  • Customer & pipeline Cold→Deal
  • Program follow-up / campaign
  • Dashboard MR & salespeople
  • Workspace percakapan MR + monitor PIC
Demo: Autopilot CRM

Social Media & Ads Automation

Dari stok live ke creative, jadwal posting, campaign insight, publish Meta Business.

Data Insight & Conversion

Insight funnel, channel, serta performa MR dan agent untuk meningkatkan conversion.

  • Lead → booking → sold

Multi-tenant SaaS: role, config cabang, pairing channel, developer API.
